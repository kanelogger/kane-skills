# Kane Q Cover Asset Usage

Read this reference only when a cover includes Kane Q.

## Reference Selection

- Use `assets/kane-q/three-view.png` as the main identity reference.
- Use `assets/kane-q/front-full-body.png` when a frontal full-body reference helps.
- Add at most one expression image when its pose or emotion supports the cover concept:
  - `01-v-sign.png`: opening, confidence, lightness
  - `02-reading.png`: reading, learning
  - `03-thinking.png`: analysis, reflection
  - `04-thumbs-up.png`: approval, recommendation
  - `05-coffee.png`: pause, recovery
  - `06-work-ready.png`: writing, execution
  - `07-shrug.png`: ambiguity, tradeoff
  - `08-starting.png`: launch, next action

Reference images establish identity. They do not limit the cover to an existing pose, crop, background, prop, rendering style, or group composition.

## Compact Character Cue

Depict Kane Q as a chibi adult male with a large head and small body, short black hair, black square-rounded glasses, and a relaxed, intelligent expression. His familiar outfit is a white short-sleeve T-shirt with a small left-chest `Kane.` mark, dark casual pants, and light sneakers. Preserve enough of these cues to keep him recognizable while adapting the cover style and concept.

## Prompt Snippet

```text
Use the bundled Kane Q three-view as the character reference. Place Kane Q in the role and action described by this cover concept, keeping his recognizable face, glasses, proportions, and familiar outfit. Adapt the pose, crop, scale, environment, interactions, and rendering to support the focal idea and title hierarchy.
```

Other people may appear normally. Do not apply Kane Q's reference image or identity cues to them.
